Educational prototype notice
This dashboard is for student demonstration purposes only. The MAX30102 does not directly measure respiratory rate or temperature. Respiratory rate requires a validated measurement or estimation method, and temperature requires a suitable separate sensor. Optical readings can be inaccurate, particularly in newborns. Do not use this dashboard to diagnose or make medical decisions. Seek professional medical help for concerns about a baby.
